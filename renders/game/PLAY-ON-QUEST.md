# House Blaster: play on the Quest

**Link:** https://192.168.1.131:8443/game.html

1. Put the headset on (same Wi-Fi as the Mac) and open the **Quest browser**.
2. Type in the address above. If you get a security warning, tap **Advanced → Proceed**.
3. Wait for the house to load, then press **Play in VR**.
4. Point the blaster at **START** on the floating menu and pull the trigger.

Trigger = fire · left stick = walk · right stick left/right = turn, forward = aim a jump, let go = jump · B/Y = menu · 🧸 Toy mode is on the menu.
