# Public terrain source

Contains Environment Agency information © Environment Agency and/or database right. Released under the Open Government Licence v3.0: https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/ .

The packaged DTM and DSM are bounded 1 m public-raster crops in EPSG:27700, with ODN heights. Saved WCS request URLs sit beside them in inputs/public-lidar; the source coverage records identify the 2019 acquisition. Metadata: https://www.data.gov.uk/dataset/01b3ee39-da3f-47b6-83da-dc98e73a461f/lidar-composite-dtm-2022-1m and https://environment.data.gov.uk/survey . Cell size is not survey accuracy; verify registration, source accuracy and changes since acquisition. No prior agent registration coefficients or fitted house levels are supplied. The 2016 half-metre archive was identified but is NOT applied or packaged.
