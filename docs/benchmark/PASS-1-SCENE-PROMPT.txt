PASS 1 OF THE STAGED BENCHMARK — THE WHOLE SITE, EXACTLY RIGHT (Jack, revised 7 November 2026)

Open ~/Desktop/Garden Studio Benchmark as your workspace. Treat this as a paying client's home that you have never seen:
everything you build must come from the supplied sources, and everything must be checked before I see it.

This pass has ONE goal: reconstruct the property and its surroundings, Before and After, exactly: the house AND the garden,
which matters almost as much as the house. Nothing else: no furniture, no planting detail, no lighting design, no editors,
no VR. A perfect plain scene is a pass. A beautiful scene with a wall in the wrong place, a missing room or an invented
object is a fail.

READ FIRST
- AS-BUILT-NOTES.md: the owner's facts about this property that the drawings don't show. They outrank the drawings.
- source-manifest.json and SOURCE-POLICY.md: the drawings, photos, videos, scans and LiDAR you may use. Check every file is
  actually readable (iCloud placeholders may need downloading); list anything you could not read.
- inputs/prepared/source-register.json: every supplied file with its capture date, and frames from every video at one per
  second with their timestamps (inputs/prepared/frames/). Start your register from it; take full-resolution frames from
  the original videos for camera solves.
- PLAN-DATA-SCHEMA.md: the format your plan is exported in, so it can be checked by machine.
- lessons/ (Claude's and Codex's mistakes) and BUILD-ENGINE-RULE.md. Turn every applicable lesson into a check.
- Do not open any earlier entrant's house, layout, renders, plans or reports.

THE METHOD (in order; each step has a check that must pass before the next)

1. Source register. List every drawing (number, revision, date, existing or proposed, superseded or current), every photo
   and every video (capture date from metadata, what it shows). Sources disagree because properties change over time:
   resolve by DATE. "Today" is the latest capture of each area; earlier captures count only for what has not changed since.
   Record, for every element you build, which source and date it came from.

2. One site frame. The plot boundary, ground levels (drawings, site plan, LiDAR), north, and the footprints of the house,
   attached and neighbouring buildings, road and pavement, in one coordinate system. Everything is built in this frame;
   never build the house and its surroundings separately.

3. Drawings to data. Read every element, not just the obvious ones: thick and thin (stud) walls, the wall below and above
   every window (sill walls and lintels), chimney breasts and the walls between them, openings (width, height, sill, head,
   door swing), floors and ceilings per storey, stairs (direction, rise, going, headroom), roofs, eaves and verges. Make a
   room inventory from the drawings' labels: every room, cupboard, landing and stair. Check the scale against at least
   three written dimensions and the owner's tape measurements, and draw the plan back over each sheet as proof.

   GATE 1A (2D) — STOP AND PRESENT: the source register, the floor plans and site plan overlaid on every drawing sheet,
   the room inventory, the scale residuals, the shared-check report, the independent review (below) with your fixes, and every
   uncertainty. Include ONE batch of genuine questions for me, like a
   client call (only what the sources cannot settle; each with your recommended answer). Wait for my approval and answers
   before building anything in 3D.

4. Things the drawings don't show (as-built changes, conversions, garden) come from dated photos and video, measured:
   solve the camera positions (structure-from-motion, or against known geometry) for enough frames to cover every area,
   at full resolution, with scale from known lengths. Never estimate or type in positions by eye.

5. Build the scene so it is easy to judge, Before and After: not textured yet, but every surface in its real colour and
   finish so walls, floors, ceilings, doors, frames and fittings read clearly (painted plaster, timber or carpet floors,
   tiled areas, white ceilings, brick and roof tiles outside in their colours). Stairs complete with treads, risers,
   strings and handrails; doors and windows with frames, glass and sills; skirting where it belongs. Build: the house, attached and neighbouring buildings (real roof shapes
   and facade openings), road, pavement and kerbs, and the garden as it is today: every path, edge, bed, lawn, wall, fence,
   hedge, step, structure and tree that the sources show.

6. Machine checks (computed, not eyeballed), Before and After:
   - At GATE 1A and again at GATE 1B, export the plan as plan-data.json in the PLAN-DATA-SCHEMA.md format and run the
     shared checks: python3 shared-tools/checks/run_checks.py plan-data.json --gate 1A (at 1B: --gate 1B with
     --overlays). Keep the report; every FAIL is fixed before you present.
   - Closure: every room in the inventory is a closed space with the drawing's door count; no missing wall, no gap in any
     wall, floor or ceiling, every opening has wall above and below it as drawn, the structure is watertight per storey.
   - Circulation: every stair rises the right way, keeps its headroom and lands clear; no stair, door swing or fitted item
     blocks another stair, door or route; every room is reachable.
   - Evidence: every built object, inside and out, cites its source; anything not seen in a dated source is not built.
     No invented hedges, walls, fences or features.
   - Overlays at full resolution, for the drawings and for every solved photo or frame: camera RMS at most 8 px; every
     structural edge within 15 px. Hide other buildings when checking an elevation so nothing blocks it.
   A failed check is fixed at its cause and re-run. Never explain a failure away in a caption.

7. Self-review. Walk every room and every part of the outside: cameras at 1.6 m in each room's centre, two opposite views
   per room, every room in both versions, and views of the garden from the same positions as the photos and video frames.
   Look at every view yourself and log every fault with its cause before fixing it.

TOOLS AND LIMITS
Any engine or tool you judge best (Blender via MCP or headless, or another), with every step scripted so the scene rebuilds
exactly from the data. The Mac is an 8 GB M1 Air: one heavy job at a time, no Cycles renders or light bakes. No spending,
logins, purchases or uploads of private material without my approval.

INDEPENDENT REVIEW (before presenting at GATE 1A, and again at GATE 1B)
Start a separate reviewer agent with a fresh context. Give it the gate's output, the original sources, the shared checks
and lessons/, but not your reasoning or excuses. It checks the output against the sources and lists every fault with its
evidence. Log each finding in MISTAKES.md, fix it at its cause, re-run the affected checks, and include the reviewer's
report and your fixes in the presentation. If your environment has no subagents, do a separate review pass from scratch
instead, and say so.

GATE 1B (3D) — STOP AND PRESENT
Only when every check passes, STOP and present: the source register, the plan overlays on each sheet, the photo and video
overlays with their residuals, the closure, circulation and evidence check results (the shared-check report), the
independent review with your fixes, the walk-round of every room and the
outside (Before and After), and every assumption with the evidence behind it. Do not start any other pass until I approve.

Keep a short PLAN.md (goal, steps, the check for each) and re-read it at every step and before you present. Log every
mistake in MISTAKES.md with its cause and the rule that prevents it before fixing it. Work in your own run folder and git
