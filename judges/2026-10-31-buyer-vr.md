# Judge 3 — buyer + VR presence (31 Oct, after scanned trees + planting)

Verdict: below both benchmarks. Only the scanned trunks/bark convince. The modelled house would
make a buyer doubt the company.

## A. Buyer
1. House is cardboard — never show the modelled house again; in the real demo the house is the
   scan, with modelled reflective glass behind real window openings; straightened facade photos
   on measured geometry if the scan melts.
2. Planting reads as weeds — plants only in beds from a planting plan, drifts of 3/5/7, three
   tiers, no bare soil; names and year-1/year-3 sizes on tap.
3. Trees wrong for Britain and twinned — multi-stem birch/amelanchier/acer; real garden's own
   trees must be recognisably the owner's.
4. Cheap materials and bugs — slatted cedar fence, real deck product, grass masked from hard
   surfaces, path 900 mm house→studio, paving with joints and no repeat.
5. Hero product not sold — furnish the studio, "from the desk" viewpoint, dusk glow shot.
6. Times of day are decoration — real sun by date with true north; night lighting plan.
7. What the scan must get right — visible tape check ("gate 1.02 m measured / in model"),
   sharp facade with glass, exact boundaries incl. neighbours' windows, same plants, levels,
   drains/manholes/meters/bins; no melted hedges, ghost cars, missing roof, photographer's
   shadow. Blur plates and faces. A studio in a FRONT garden is not permitted development
   (forward of the principal elevation); >5 m² undrained hard surface needs permission —
   design the front garden as a front garden or say it is a demo placement.
8. The overlay that makes a buyer sign — Today/Proposed wipe from the same spot, marked-out
   stage, dimensions on demand, every item specced and priced, what you lose in numbers,
   sun study, planning/drainage notes, printable plan, clear real-vs-proposed split, the film.

## B. VR
1. Desktop look will not reach the headset (no post in WebXR) — bake AO/haze/glow into
   materials and lightmaps; judge the Quest build only from in-headset captures.
2. Foliage will crawl in stereo — alpha-to-coverage + 4x MSAA, coverage-preserving mips,
   shadow camera snapped to texels (done), real geometry <3 m, impostors >12 m.
3. Night/golden go to mud on LCD — deep blue not black, dither, bright anchors, lawn >10% lum.
4. Sky at finite radius shows parallax; HDRI horizon trees zero-parallax — sky at infinity
   (centre on head each frame); for the real garden a 360° panorama from mid-garden as far field.
5. Audio is the wrong place for a street-front garden — use the capture's audio track,
   positioned sources (HRTF), surface-aware footsteps, haptic tick off by default.
6. Comfort — fade stage changes 0.5 s and move the player out of new solids; drop the body blob
   when a sun shadow exists; smoothed walk surface under the head for physical walking.
7. Measure first on Quest — IPD, worst view, OVR Metrics (GPU ms, FPS, stale frames), draw
   calls/tris on the wrist panel vs 150/500k budget, toggles per feature with ms cost, time to
   first frame, three reloads (texture memory), 15-minute thermal walk.
8. First in-headset test of the scan — floor ±2 cm at three points; in-app tape, ≤1% error on
   three real-tape distances; holes walk; texture: brick mortar readable at 1 m (needs ~2 mm/texel
   → tiled 4k KTX2 + detail blend); sky seam turn at centre and corners; set sun to capture time
   first; ghosts; collide against a simplified walk mesh.

Urgent for today's capture: overcast, own shadow out, two markers 2.000 m apart, ground at
feet from waist and eye height, tops of hedges/walls, one 360° panorama from the middle,
3 minutes of ambient audio, cars moved.

Most moves the sale: own house and garden checked against a tape, wipe to the proposal, priced,
real-date sun study. Most moves presence: ground locked to reality, 72–90 fps measured.
