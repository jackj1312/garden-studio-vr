## Top faults a client sees in the first 60 seconds (worst first)

1. **Stale collision, so you walk through new things.** `site.json` was last written 31 Oct 14:36, before audit-2. The upright-shrub obstacle is still a 0.5 x 1.1 m box at z 0 (z -0.55..0.55). The new hedge runs z 0.6 to -3.05, so about 2.5 m of it, the exact thing Jack asked for, is walk-through. Also uncollided: the bench, the two conifers, the new wing bed, the shed's new roof overhang and the bins' new positions. Collision is boxes and circles only, so the curved bed is a rectangle (`planted bed and pergola front posts`, x -1.8..4.46, z 0.95..2.93). You will be blocked 30 cm short of the border in some spots and pass through plants in others.
2. **Conifers are orange and green cones with visible facets.** Sheets 01, 02, 03, 08, 12: the largest saturated object by the door is a cartoon traffic cone beside a spiral topiary in a patterned pot. In VR it is at arm's length. Jack will see it before the door.
3. **Bench is a plain grey box.** The real one is a curved stone bench with carved ends and pumpkins. Both are "today" extras, but they sit on the step you arrive at.
4. **Planting is stylised.** Violet sticks for lavender, faceted dark-green balls (sheets 05, 06, 04) at 0.3 to 1 m in a headset. Plus the vegetation textures are repeated-card grass (grass tufts reduced to 160 per m2 on Quest). Real beds are muted greens, pinks, grey-green lavender. This is the largest remaining "not my garden" cue; AUDIT.md "Not resolved" lists it.
5. **Apple tree trunk is a gnarled black stub; the crown is small.** The real tree dominates the left of walk_0017 and walk_0059. Open item 2 in AUDIT.md.
6. **Surroundings are flat.** Lawn runs to the horizon as one olive plane at y -0.08 (radius 160 m); the real view has the drive, car, houses and trees. Only a fence/wall in the middle distance. The viewer stands on a flat plane under a CG sky.
7. **Lighting differs from the photos.** The model sun is a low 22 degree, deep-blue-sky look, and hard shadow edges on slats and posts at 1024 shadow map (about 1.8 cm per texel, fine). The shed face and pergola read darker and browner than the real pale pine.
8. **Start point.** Start is x -4.4, z 17, 17 m from the house at 1.4 m/s: about 12 seconds of walking before anything is close. That is acceptable, but a client first sees the house from a distance with the street hedge at z 19.4 right behind them.
9. **Shed face.** A pale translucent ghost appears in the model over the shed door (the chalk smudge on the door rendered pale blue) and a lighter patch near the neighbour fence (sheet_07). Minor; check on close approach.

## Performance and comfort risks

- **Quest cut `model_q.glb` is 11 MB but heavy once decoded.** Draco and WebP: about 181 k triangles, 504 meshes (merged by material in `site.js`, so about 57 draws), 8.7 MB of WebP textures expanding to roughly 150-250 MB of GPU memory. Full `model.glb` (27 MB, 344 k triangles, 23.8 MB of textures) is the desktop build; a Quest that loads it by mistake will drop frames. The UA test is `/OculusBrowser|Quest/`; fine, but untested on a real device in this review.
- **Load time.** Draco decode of 11 MB plus 34 WebP decodes on the Quest CPU, then a 10 cm lawn raster and grass instancing, is probably 15-30 s of black screen. Fade-in exists after the session starts, but nothing shows progress.
- **Frame rate.** 160 tufts/m2 grass, 1024 shadow map, and the plants still cast no shadows on Quest. Foveation 0.4, framebuffer scale 1.0, no post. It will probably hold 72 fps in open lawn, but the planting beds (about 146 k of the 181 k triangles, alpha-tested, double-sided, alphaToCoverage) are where the Quest will dip, and Jack will stand right next to them.
- **Single-sided faces.** `site.js` forces FrontSide on everything that is not plant or glass. AUDIT.md says open parts are now 0, which should fix verge/soffit disappearing, but any remaining reversed winding will vanish from behind. Re-check ridge/hip tiles and the underside of the pergola rafters in the headset.
- **Eye height and scale.** `eyeHeight` 1.68 is only used in desktop mode; in the headset local-floor pose rules, so scale is right if the Quest floor is set. There is no recentre control or seated mode noted. Hands: controller models load from a CDN, so they appear late or not at all offline.
- **Comfort.** Vignette is on while moving (opacity 0.5), 30 degree snap-turn, smooth walk 1.4 m/s. No teleport option; fine for most, but smooth walking past bright slats, shadow-stripe patterns and sparse-texel foliage can induce motion cues.
- **Texture tiling.** The brick sheet and the lawn texture will tile visibly at 1 m, and paving seams in shadow read as dark lines. No sign of z-fighting after the clash fixes.

## Bottom line

Of the 13 notes, 12 would be accepted from the images; note 1 only partly. The thing that will actually break the feeling in the first minute is not the 13 notes: it is walking through the hedge he asked for, the cone-shaped conifers and bench by the door, and the cartoon planting. Fix collision (regenerate `site.json` obstacles from plan_v3) before the next headset session.
