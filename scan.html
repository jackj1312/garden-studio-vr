Room scan

Scan each room with the Quest. Pick the room, put the headset on and press Start. Every room you save is sent straight to the studio.

  1. Stand in the middle of the room and press Start.
  2. If the Quest doesn't know this room yet, press B to run room setup. Look slowly round every wall, the floor and the ceiling until the mesh covers it all.
  3. In room setup, keep the boxes for real furniture and fittings: sofa, bed, table, wardrobe, units, bath, radiator. Delete any it puts on toys, bags or mess.
  4. Back in the scan, check the coloured outlines sit on the walls, doors and windows. Then pull the right trigger to save. You'll feel a buzz and see "Saved".
  5. Press Y or the Meta button to finish, then pick the next room.

Safety: on the stairs and in the loft, stand still while scanning and move only with the headset off or with passthrough clear in front of you. In the loft, stay on boarded areas.
